# Guild Crest Rules

- Base silhouette must be exactly one of: classic shield or circle.
- Use one dominant symbol only.
- The dominant symbol must be one of: animalistic, natural, military.
- Do not mix multiple major symbol families in one crest unless explicitly requested.
- Subtle religious hints are allowed, but only as secondary painted details and never as the dominant symbol.
- Religious hints should stay grounded and restrained: halo rings, sunbursts, reliquary lines, candle marks, prayer knots, shrine borders, or other devotional paint motifs.
- The symbol must always be painted only, not made from a different material than the background.
- Base materials may include wood, painted wood, forged iron, steel, bronze, enamel, or similar grounded medieval materials, but the symbol reads as painted on that base.
- Keep the art grounded, readable, and heraldic rather than high-fantasy or overly ornate.
- No text, no lettering, no banner pole, no shield bearer, and no environment.
